### 2. THE WINNER (Calculated Result)

*   **Selected Formula:** Formula 4 (Gelatin_methacrylate(GelMA) & Silk_Fibroin(SK))
*   **One-Sentence Rationale:** Formula 4 mathematically and clinically wins because it achieves the optimal biomechanical-biochemical equilibrium, leveraging Silk Fibroin's robust beta-sheet mechanics to match arterial compliance (1.0-2.0 MPa) while utilizing GelMA's RGD motifs to drive rapid endothelialization without triggering thrombosis or lumen occlusion.

***

### 3. THE "WHY" (Detailed Logic for the Winner)

*   **Mechanical Fit:** To hit the 1.0 - 2.0 MPa target, hydrogels often require dense crosslinking, which makes them brittle. Silk Fibroin (SK) solves this via its inherent beta-sheet secondary structures, which provide immense tensile strength and toughness. By creating an interpenetrating network (IPN) of UV-crosslinked GelMA and physically crosslinked Silk Fibroin, the liner easily matches the coronary artery's Young's Modulus while maintaining the elasticity required to absorb pulsatile shear stress.
*   **Swelling Profile:** The polymer physics trade-off dictates that high swelling usually compromises mechanical integrity. GelMA alone swells significantly, but the hydrophobic domains and crystalline beta-sheets of the Silk Fibroin act as a structural constraint. This creates a "Goldilocks" swelling profile: it absorbs enough fluid to conform perfectly to the vessel wall and deliver potential therapeutics, but is physically restricted from uncontrolled volumetric expansion that would cause catastrophic lumen occlusion.

***

### 4. THE SAFETY AUDIT (Winner's Pros & Cons)

*   **Biological "Green Flags":** 
    *   *Cellular Equilibrium:* GelMA retains the RGD (Arginine-Glycine-Aspartic acid) amino acid sequences from native collagen, which are highly recognized by endothelial cell integrins, promoting rapid re-endothelialization. 
    *   *SMC-Inhibition:* Silk fibroin surfaces have been uniquely demonstrated in vascular tissue engineering to favor endothelial cell proliferation while naturally downregulating smooth muscle cell (SMC) attachment and synthetic phenotype transition, directly combating in-stent restenosis.
    *   *Physiochemical Equilibrium:* Unlike synthetic polyesters (e.g., PLA/PGA) that degrade into acidic byproducts and cause local inflammation, both GelMA and Silk degrade enzymatically into benign amino acids and peptides, maintaining a neutral local pH.
*   **Potential "Red Flags" & Phantom Factors:** 
    *   *The Sericin Phantom Factor:* Silk Fibroin is extracted from raw silk, which contains a glue-like protein called sericin. If the silk is not rigorously degummed during synthesis, residual sericin will trigger a severe macrophage-mediated foreign body response and hypersensitivity reaction. The high score assumes a perfectly purified, medical-grade silk fibroin.
    *   *GelMA Thrombogenicity:* While methacrylation consumes many of the amine groups that normally trigger platelet activation in raw gelatin, there is still a mild inherent risk of thrombogenicity. The blending with the smoother, less reactive silk fibroin mitigates this, but dual-antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) would still be strictly required post-deployment.

***

### 5. REJECTED CANDIDATES (Brief Autopsy)

*   **Rejected due to Thrombosis Risk (<4 Score):** 
    *   *Formula 1, Formula 2:* Raw Gelatin (without sufficient masking) retains too many collagen-like properties that actively promote platelet adhesion and aggregation. 
    *   *Formula 3, Formula 7:* Chitosan and Chitin are well-documented hemostatic agents. Deploying them in a coronary artery is a fatal flaw that would induce acute thrombosis.
*   **Rejected due to Endothelialization Failure (<4 Score):** 
    *   *Formula 9, Formula 10:* Polyacrylamide (PAM), Polyvinyl Alcohol (PVA), and Polyethylene Glycol (PEG) are highly hydrophilic and anti-fouling. While excellent at preventing SMCs and thrombosis, they completely prevent endothelial cell attachment. Without a confluent endothelial layer, the liner remains a permanent bare surface, leading to late-stage thrombosis.
*   **Rejected due to Mechanical/Swelling Mismatch (<4 Score):** 
    *   *Formula 6:* Starch lacks the structural integrity to provide mechanical support against uneven vessel stress and degrades unpredictably.
    *   *Formula 5:* PEG is highly hydrophilic. To prevent it from swelling uncontrollably and occluding the lumen, it must be heavily crosslinked, which pushes the material out of the target Young's Modulus and makes it dangerously brittle.
